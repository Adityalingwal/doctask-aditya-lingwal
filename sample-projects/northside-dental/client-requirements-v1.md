# Northside Dental — Client Requirements v1

Date: 10 June 2026

Prepared by: Software Provider Delivery Owner

Approved by: Clinic Practice Manager

## Scope

The provider will build online appointment booking for the Northside Dental
website, together with the reminder and schedule work listed below. This
document is the written scope the clinic has approved.

## Requirements

- Online booking — A patient must be able to book an appointment from the
  clinic website without telephoning reception.
- Appointment reminder — The system must send the patient an email reminder
  before the appointment.
- Daily schedule screen — Each dentist must see a doctor-wise list of their own
  appointments for the day.
- Cancel or reschedule link — Every booking confirmation must carry a link the
  patient can use to cancel or reschedule the appointment.

## Out of scope

Anything not listed under Requirements is out of scope for this release.
