# Intake Portal — Client Requirements v1

**Date:** 12 March 2026
**Prepared by:** Software Provider Delivery Owner
**Approved by:** Client Operations Lead

## Scope

The provider will build an intake portal for the client's operations team. The
scope below is what the client has approved in writing.

## Requirements

1. An intake form that validates every required field before it submits.
2. An email notification to the operations team whenever a visitor submits the
   intake form.
3. A records list page showing every intake record the portal has taken.

## Out of scope

Anything not listed above is out of scope for this release.
