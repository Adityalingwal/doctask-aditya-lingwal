# MediBook Clinic — Delivery Handover Summary

**Date:** 20 August 2026
**Prepared by:** Software Provider Delivery Owner

## What was handed over

Online booking is built and live on the MediBook website — a patient can
pick a clinic, a doctor and a slot, and the appointment lands in the
system straight away.

The appointment reminders are built and switched on: each patient gets an
email and an SMS ahead of their appointment.

The front-desk dashboard is delivered — reception at each clinic can see
the day's appointments for every doctor on one screen.

The monthly bookings and no-shows report is delivered and scheduled; the
first one goes out at the start of next month.

The CSV export of appointment data is delivered and can be downloaded from
the dashboard for the accounting tool.

We also built the reception tablet check-in: a patient who walks in can
tap their name on the tablet and check in without queueing at the counter.
This wasn't in the original written scope, but it came up on the first
call and was small enough to build alongside the rest.

## Who to contact

Day-to-day questions go to the MediBook Clinic Operations Manager. Anything
that needs a change to the product comes back to the Software Provider
Delivery Owner.
