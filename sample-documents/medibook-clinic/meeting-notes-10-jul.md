# MediBook Clinic — Meeting Notes

**Date:** 10 July 2026
**Attendees:** MediBook Clinic Operations Manager, Software Provider Delivery Owner

## Discussion

First call with MediBook, a chain of three walk-in clinics, about their
appointment product. MediBook wants online appointment booking on the clinic
website, so patients can pick a doctor and a slot without phoning the front
desk.

They also want automatic appointment reminders sent to patients — both an
email and an SMS — because missed appointments are their biggest daily
problem.

The operations manager wants a front-desk dashboard that shows the day's
appointments for each doctor in one screen, so the reception team stops
printing the schedule every morning.

Patients must also be able to cancel or reschedule their appointment online
themselves, instead of calling the clinic to do it.

They asked for a monthly report of bookings and no-shows per clinic, so the
operations manager can compare the three locations without building the
numbers by hand.

Right at the end of the call, the operations manager asked for one more
thing: a small tablet app for the reception desk, so a patient who walks in
can tap their name and check in without waiting at the counter. "The queue
at reception is the first thing a patient sees — we need that check-in on a
tablet," she said. It came up only in conversation and was never written
into a formal ask.

The Software Provider Delivery Owner will turn this into a written scope and
send it back for sign-off.
