# MediBook Clinic — Client Requirements v1

**Date:** 15 July 2026
**Prepared by:** Software Provider Delivery Owner
**Approved by:** MediBook Clinic Operations Manager

## Scope

The provider will build an appointment product for MediBook's three walk-in
clinics. The scope below is what MediBook has approved in writing.

## Requirements

1. Online appointment booking on the MediBook website — a patient picks a
   clinic, a doctor and a slot.
2. Automatic appointment reminders to patients, sent as both an email and an
   SMS ahead of the appointment.
3. A front-desk dashboard showing the day's appointments for each doctor at
   the chosen clinic.
4. Patients can cancel or reschedule their own appointment online.
5. A monthly report of bookings and no-shows for each of the three clinics.
6. An export of appointment data as a CSV file, so the clinic's accounting
   tool can read it.

## Out of scope

Anything not listed above is out of scope for this release.
