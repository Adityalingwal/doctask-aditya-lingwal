# MediBook Clinic — Testing Feedback

**Date:** 5 September 2026
**Tested by:** MediBook Clinic Operations Manager

## What we tried

We ran the new appointment product at all three clinics for two weeks —
booking real appointments through the website, watching the reminders,
using the front-desk dashboard every morning, and checking the tablets at
reception.

## What we found

Online booking works as asked — patients booked through the website at all
three clinics and every appointment showed up correctly.

The reminders are half working. The email reminder arrives on time for
every appointment, but the SMS reminder has never been sent — not one
patient has received a text in two weeks.

The front-desk dashboard passed our testing. Reception at each clinic used
it every morning and the day's appointments were always correct.

We could not find any way for a patient to cancel or reschedule online.
There is nothing in the patient's booking confirmation or on the website
that offers it, so patients are still calling the front desk to change
appointments.

The reception tablet check-in is missing entirely. There is no check-in
app on any of the tablets at the three clinics, and walk-in patients are
still queueing at the counter.

One new thing we want: patients should also get a WhatsApp reminder, not
just email and SMS, since many of our patients respond fastest there.
Logging this here so it is not lost.
