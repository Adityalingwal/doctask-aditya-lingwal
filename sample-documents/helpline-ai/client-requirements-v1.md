# Helpline AI — Client Requirements v1

**Date:** 5 July 2026
**Prepared by:** Software Provider Delivery Owner
**Approved by:** BrightCart Operations Lead

## Scope

The provider will build an AI customer-support product for BrightCart. The
scope below is what BrightCart has approved in writing.

## Requirements

1. A voice agent that answers incoming calls to BrightCart's support line and
   handles the customer's question directly.
2. A chat widget on the BrightCart website that acts as a support bot.
3. The same support bot made available to customers on WhatsApp.
4. A transcripts dashboard showing every support call and every chat in one
   place.
5. A weekly analytics report covering call and chat volumes and how many of
   each were resolved.
6. Support in both Hindi and English — the bot must understand and answer in
   whichever language the customer uses.

## Out of scope

Anything not listed above is out of scope for this release.
