# Helpline AI — Delivery Handover Summary

**Date:** 5 August 2026
**Prepared by:** Software Provider Delivery Owner

## What was handed over

The voice agent is built and live — it now answers BrightCart's support line
directly and handles the call without a human picking up first.

The chat widget is also built and live on the BrightCart website.

We also built a "talk to a human" handover: when the bot can't resolve a
chat, it now hands the conversation across to a live agent instead of
leaving the customer stuck. This wasn't in the original written scope, but
it came up on the first call and felt important enough to build alongside
the rest.

The transcripts dashboard is partly there — chat transcripts are showing up
correctly, but call transcripts aren't ready yet. We're still working on
pulling those out of the voice agent and will follow up once they're in.

The weekly analytics report is delivered and running; the first report went
out to the ops lead this week.

## Who to contact

Day-to-day questions go to the BrightCart Operations Lead. Anything that
needs a change to the bot or the dashboard comes back to the Software
Provider Delivery Owner.
